Privacy

This website has no ads and no tracking. The game keeps your settings (keys, graphics, phone button layout) in your browser.

Accounts. You only need an account for Quick Match. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us a number that identifies your Google account; we don't ask for or keep your e-mail address, your real name or your picture. We keep that number, the nickname you pick, your online stats (wins, losses, blocks, damage, matches) and your game settings, so they follow you to other devices. Your nickname is shown to the players you meet online. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

Feedback. Messages you send with the FEEDBACK button (bugs, improvements, ideas) are read only by the developer, together with your nickname, the game version and the kind of device. You can send one message every 24 hours. If the button is misused, the developer can stop an account from sending more; that block is kept with your Google account number, even if you delete the account.

The account is kept on Cloudflare's servers. You can delete it at any time in the game (Account, then Delete account): that removes your nickname, stats, settings and any feedback you sent for good. Questions: crossguardstudios@gmail.com.
