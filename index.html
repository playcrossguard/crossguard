Crossguard

A fast, hand-drawn fighting game. Pick one of 50 fighters, aim your ultimate, and win two rounds before they do.

▶  Play free How to play

Runs in your browser · Needs a keyboard · 1 or 2 players

Every fight is short and loud

Matches are best of 3, so a game takes a few minutes. Learn a fighter's timing, block at the right moment to stun your attacker, and save your ultimate for the opening.

50original fighters, each with their own attack and ultimate
16arenas, from a beach lagoon to an airship above the clouds
9ultimates you aim yourself, up, down or behind you
2players on one keyboard, or you against the CPU

Aim the big one

When the gold bar fills, your ultimate is ready. Nine of them stop for a moment and let you aim: an arrow appears, and you tilt it before you let go.

  • The Lancer lunges straight into the air, then drops lance-first onto whoever is below.
  • The Wind Duelist hurls a cyclone that leaves you dizzy.
  • The Staff Monk's staff shoots out across half the arena.
  • Original music, and a short tutorial the first time you play.

On the fighter screen: Q switches between vs the CPU and 2 players, 1 2 3 4 set the CPU's difficulty (4 is Crazy), H opens How to Play. In a match, Enter opens the menu and M mutes.
